# Aurora Jewels — Scope & Quote

**Project:** Premium Pakistan-First Jewelry E-Commerce Website
**Document Type:** Scope & Quote
**Prepared From:** Aurora Jewels Product Discovery Analysis
**Target Launch:** Approximately 4–6 weeks, subject to client readiness and integration dependencies

---

## 1. Project Summary

Aurora Jewels requires a premium, Pakistan-first e-commerce website for selling jewelry online. The experience should position Aurora Jewels as a sophisticated, timeless and exclusive jewelry house rather than a generic online retailer.

The website will combine premium editorial presentation, strong product merchandising, e-commerce functionality, restrained motion, and business-managed content and commerce operations.

The confirmed launch scope includes approximately **50–100 products**, Pakistani delivery, COD and online payments, guest checkout, optional customer accounts, wishlist and reviews, together with an administrative system for managing products, inventory, orders, customers, discounts, reviews and website content.

The project is targeted for an approximately **4–6 week launch window**, provided required content, product data, approvals, payment/courier onboarding and other client dependencies are available on time.

---

## 2. Objectives

The project objectives are to:

1. Generate e-commerce sales through the Aurora Jewels website.
2. Establish Aurora Jewels as a premium jewelry brand.
3. Create a sophisticated and luxurious digital brand experience.
4. Present products clearly and attractively to support purchasing decisions.
5. Provide customers with an easy way to discover and purchase jewelry.
6. Support Pakistan-first payment and delivery requirements.
7. Allow guest purchasing alongside optional customer accounts.
8. Allow routine product, pricing, inventory, order and content changes without developer involvement.
9. Provide a scalable foundation for future catalog and customer growth.
10. Launch professionally within approximately 4–6 weeks.

# 5. Design Scope

The design direction will follow the confirmed Aurora Jewels positioning:

* Premium
* Minimal
* Luxurious
* Editorial
* Sophisticated
* Timeless
* Elegant rather than flashy

### Visual Direction

The design will make use of:

* Large editorial/product photography
* Sophisticated typography
* Generous whitespace
* Strong product presentation
* Premium visual hierarchy
* Storytelling-led homepage composition
* A visual direction incorporating deep emerald/teal, champagne/gold, ivory and black

Final color and typography specifications remain subject to design development and client approval.

### UX Principles

The design will balance:

* Brand storytelling
* Product discovery
* Product presentation
* Shopping efficiency
* Checkout usability
* Performance

The premium visual treatment will not intentionally compromise the core shopping journey.

# 13. Revision Policy

The project will use milestone-based review and approval.

### Included Revisions

Design and implementation revisions will be handled within the agreed project scope and based on consolidated client feedback.

Client feedback should be provided as one consolidated set of comments per review round wherever possible.

### Out-of-Scope Revisions

The following may require a scope change or additional quotation:

* New pages not listed in the included scope
* New functionality
* New integrations
* Major changes to an already approved design direction
* New user roles/workflows
* Significant changes to approved content structure
* Repeated redesign caused by changed requirements
* Features previously identified as future/optional

Final revision limits or the number of formal design review rounds should be confirmed in the commercial agreement.
