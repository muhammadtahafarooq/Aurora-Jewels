Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our collections, materials, and services. If you need further assistance, our concierges are always available.

We offer complimentary express shipping globally. All pieces are fully insured during transit and require a signature upon delivery to ensure security.

Returns or exchanges are accepted within 30 days of receipt, provided the piece is in its original, unworn condition with all tags and certification attached. Custom or engraved items are final sale.

Our jewelry is crafted from solid 18k gold and platinum, utilizing only the highest grade precious stones. We recommend having your settings checked annually by our specialists.

To clean, gently wipe with the provided polishing cloth. Avoid exposing pieces to harsh chemicals, extreme temperatures, or wearing them during rigorous activities.

Aurora Jewels is committed to zero-impact sourcing. Every diamond is conflict-free and traceable to its mine of origin. We exclusively partner with suppliers who adhere to strict environmental and labor standards, ensuring the beauty of our pieces never comes at a human or ecological cost.

We offer private appointments at our flagship boutiques or virtual consultations via secure video link. Our gemologists can guide you through bespoke commissions, legacy repairs, or private viewings of unreleased collections.

Still have questions?

Our client advisors are available to assist you with inquiries regarding styling, fit, or bespoke commissions.
