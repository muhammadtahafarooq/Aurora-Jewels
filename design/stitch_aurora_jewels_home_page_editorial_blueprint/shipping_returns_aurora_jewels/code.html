Shipping & Returns

We ensure that every piece is delivered with the same care and precision with which it was crafted. Our logistics partners are selected for their commitment to secure, refined delivery.

Shipping

Domestic Delivery (Pakistan)

We offer complimentary domestic shipping on all orders within Pakistan. Deliveries are typically fulfilled within 3-5 business days from the date of dispatch. All domestic shipments are fully insured and require a signature upon receipt to guarantee security.

International Shipping

For our international clientele, we partner with premier global courier services. Delivery timeframes vary by destination, generally ranging from 7-14 business days. Please note that all international orders are subject to local customs duties and taxes, which are the responsibility of the recipient.

Order Tracking

Upon dispatch, you will receive a confirmation email containing a unique tracking reference. This allows you to monitor the progress of your delivery via our courier partner's secure portal.

Returns

Return Policy & Eligibility

We accept returns on pristine, unworn items within 14 days of receipt. To be eligible for a return, the piece must remain in its original condition, with all packaging and authenticity documentation intact. Custom or personalized pieces are final sale and not eligible for return.

The Process

  1. 01
    Initiate Return Contact our Client Care team to request a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number.
  2. 02
    Secure Packaging Carefully pack the item in its original presentation box and shipping carton.
  3. 03
    Dispatch Await the courier collection arranged by our team or drop off at the designated secure location.
  4. 04
    Inspection & Refund Upon receipt and inspection by our quality assurance team, approved refunds will be processed to the original payment method.

Need Assistance?

Our advisors are available to guide you through the delivery or returns process with utmost discretion.
